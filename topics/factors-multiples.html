Grade 4

Factors & Multiples.

Factors divide evenly into a number. Multiples are what you get by multiplying. Prime numbers are the ones with only two factors.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Find all factor pairs of a number

    Numbers that multiply to give it.

  • 02
    Find multiples

    The times table of a number — never ends.

  • 03
    Tell prime from composite

    Prime: only 1 and itself. Composite: more than two factors.

  • 04
    Spot common factors

    Numbers that show up in both lists.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
List all factors of 24.
1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24.
Is 17 prime or composite?
Prime — only 1 and 17 work.
First five multiples of 6?
6, 12, 18, 24, 30.
