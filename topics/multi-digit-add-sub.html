Multi-Digit Addition & Subtraction.

Working with numbers up to six digits. Same algorithm, more columns — line them up by place value and trust the process.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Add and subtract numbers up to six digits

    Use the standard algorithm.

  • 02
    Estimate first

    Round both numbers, then check your exact answer against it.

  • 03
    Solve multi-step word problems

    Sometimes you add, sometimes subtract, sometimes both.

  • 04
    Borrow across multiple zeros

    When subtracting from numbers like 5,000 — borrow once and pass it down.
