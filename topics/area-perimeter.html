Grade 3

Area & Perimeter.

Perimeter is the trip around the outside. Area is the space inside. Same shape, two different questions.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Find perimeter by adding the sides

    Walk around the shape — add each side as you go.

  • 02
    Count squares to find area

    How many unit squares fit inside.

  • 03
    Use multiplication for rectangle area

    Length × width gives the total.

  • 04
    Tell when a problem wants area vs. perimeter

    Fence around a yard is perimeter. Carpet inside is area.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
A rectangle is 4 cm by 7 cm. Area?
28 sq cm.
Same rectangle. Perimeter?
22 cm.
A square has side 5 m. Area?
25 sq m.
Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Mixing up the formulas

Multiplying for perimeter or adding for area.

02

Forgetting square units for area

Writing "28 cm" instead of "28 sq cm".
