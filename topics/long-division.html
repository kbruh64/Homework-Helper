Grade 4

Long Division.

Dividing big numbers by a single-digit divisor. Four steps repeated: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Divide a 3 or 4-digit number by a 1-digit number

    Work left to right.

  • 02
    Run the four-step cycle

    Divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — repeat until done.

  • 03
    Interpret the remainder in context

    Sometimes round up, sometimes leave it, sometimes share more.

  • 04
    Estimate the quotient first

    Helps you catch a wrong digit early.

Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Dropping the bring-down step

Skipping a digit makes the answer the wrong size.

02

Misreading the remainder

Saying "5 buses needed for 17 kids if each holds 4" but answering 4.
