Grade 4

Lines, Angles & Triangles.

Points, lines, line segments, rays, and the angles they make. Triangles get classified by both their sides and their angles.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Tell points, lines, segments, and rays apart

    Each one has a different definition.

  • 02
    Recognize parallel and perpendicular lines

    Parallel never meet. Perpendicular cross at 90°.

  • 03
    Classify triangles by angles

    Acute, right, obtuse.

  • 04
    Classify triangles by sides

    Equilateral, isosceles, scalene.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
A triangle with all sides equal is called?
Equilateral.
Two lines that meet at 90° are?
Perpendicular.
A triangle has angles 90°, 45°, 45°. Type?
Right and isosceles.
