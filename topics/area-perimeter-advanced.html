Grade 4

Area & Perimeter (Advanced).

Using formulas, working with composite shapes, and solving real word problems. The same two ideas as Grade 3, just more layered.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Use the rectangle area formula

    Length × width.

  • 02
    Find the perimeter of any polygon

    Add up every side.

  • 03
    Break composite shapes into rectangles

    Find each piece, then add or subtract.

  • 04
    Solve word problems with area and perimeter

    Decide which one the situation needs.
