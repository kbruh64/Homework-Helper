Grade 4

Measurement Conversions.

Switching between units in the same system — cm to m, g to kg, mL to L. Multiply or divide by powers of ten.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Convert between metric units

    100 cm in a meter. 1,000 g in a kilogram.

  • 02
    Solve multi-step measurement problems

    Convert first, then add or subtract.

  • 03
    Measure angles in degrees

    Use a protractor — start at zero, follow the arc.

  • 04
    Classify angles

    Acute, right, obtuse, straight.
