Division.

Division splits a total into equal groups. It is the inverse of multiplication — and that link is the key to remembering facts.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    See division as sharing equally

    12 ÷ 3 means twelve shared into three groups.

  • 02
    Use multiplication to check

    If 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 3 should give 12.

  • 03
    Know division facts to 100 ÷ 10

    Work backward from the times tables.

  • 04
    Notice when there are leftovers

    A remainder means the share was not even.

Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Dividing in the wrong direction

Doing 6 ÷ 24 instead of 24 ÷ 6.

02

Ignoring remainders

Saying 13 ÷ 4 = 3 and forgetting the leftover.
