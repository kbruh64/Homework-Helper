Grade 4

Decimals.

Decimals are fractions with denominators of 10 or 100. The decimal point is just a marker for where ones end.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Connect decimals to fractions

    0.5 is 1/2. 0.25 is 1/4.

  • 02
    Read decimals to the hundredths

    Tenths, then hundredths — each place is ten times smaller.

  • 03
    Compare decimals

    Line up the decimal points and look from the left.

  • 04
    Add and subtract decimals

    Stack them by the decimal point, fill missing places with zeros.
