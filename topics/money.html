Money.

Coins, bills, totals, and making change. Money is just decimals dressed up — but the practical version comes first.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Identify coins and bills

    Know each value at a glance.

  • 02
    Count mixed coins and bills

    Start with the largest values.

  • 03
    Make change

    Count up from the price to the amount given.

  • 04
    Solve money word problems

    Most are just addition and subtraction.

Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Counting smallest coins first

Slows everything down and causes errors.

02

Mixing up dimes and pennies

They are similar in size but worth ten times different.
