Grade 3

Time.

Reading clocks, knowing AM from PM, and figuring out how long something took. Elapsed time is the part most kids find tricky.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Tell time to the nearest minute

    On both digital and analog clocks.

  • 02
    Use AM and PM

    AM is morning. PM is afternoon and evening.

  • 03
    Find elapsed time

    Count forward from the start to the end.

  • 04
    Solve time word problems

    Sketch a number line if it helps.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
A movie starts at 2:15 and ends at 3:40. How long is it?
1 hour 25 minutes.
School starts at 8:30 AM. Lunch is 4 hours later. When?
12:30 PM.
Read 7:48 — how many minutes to 8:00?
12 minutes.
Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Treating time like base-10

Doing 3:40 − 2:15 = 1:25 by subtracting columns is correct, but 3:10 − 2:45 trips many kids up.

02

Crossing AM/PM without noticing

Forgetting that 11 AM to 2 PM is 3 hours.
