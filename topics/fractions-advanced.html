Grade 4

Fractions (Advanced).

Adding and subtracting with like denominators, comparing with unlike ones, and converting between mixed numbers and improper fractions.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Add and subtract fractions with the same bottom

    Just add or subtract the tops.

  • 02
    Compare fractions with different bottoms

    Find a common denominator first.

  • 03
    Convert between mixed numbers and improper fractions

    Same amount, two ways to write it.

  • 04
    Multiply a fraction by a whole number

    3 × 1/4 means three groups of one-fourth.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
2/5 + 1/5 = ?
3/5
Write 7/3 as a mixed number.
2 1/3
Which is larger: 3/4 or 5/8?
3/4 (which is 6/8).
Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Adding the bottoms too

Saying 1/4 + 1/4 = 2/8.

02

Comparing without a common denominator

Guessing 3/5 < 1/2 because 3 < 5.
