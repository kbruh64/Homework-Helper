Grade 3

Place Value.

Numbers up to 1,000 are made of hundreds, tens, and ones. Knowing where each digit lives is the start of every operation.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Read and write numbers up to 1,000

    In digits and in words.

  • 02
    Identify the place of a digit

    Hundreds, tens, ones — and what each one is worth.

  • 03
    Compare and order numbers

    Use place value to decide which is larger.

  • 04
    Round to the nearest 10 or 100

    Look one place to the right to decide.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
What is the value of 4 in 642?
40 — it is in the tens place.
Round 287 to the nearest 100.
300
Which is larger: 519 or 591?
591
Common slip-ups

The mistakes most kids make here.

Knowing the trap is half of avoiding it. If you catch yourself doing one of these, slow down — it just means the concept is still settling.

01

Reading the digit, not the place

Saying the 4 in 642 is worth "4" instead of 40.

02

Rounding the wrong direction

Rounding 287 to 200 because the 2 is smaller.
