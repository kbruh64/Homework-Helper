Grade 3

Measurement.

Length, mass, and capacity — the standard units and when to use them. Estimating before measuring builds number sense.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Use cm, m, and km for length

    A pencil in cm. A hallway in m. A drive in km.

  • 02
    Use g and kg for mass

    A grape in grams. A backpack in kilograms.

  • 03
    Use mL and L for liquids

    A spoonful in mL. A juice bottle in L.

  • 04
    Pick the right unit

    Big things use big units. Small things use small.

Try these

Quick examples

Read the question, think it through, then peek at the answer.
What unit measures the length of a car?
Meters (m).
Estimate the mass of an apple.
About 150 grams.
Which is bigger: 1 L or 750 mL?
1 L (it is 1,000 mL).
