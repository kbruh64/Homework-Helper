Grade 3

Addition & Subtraction.

Adding and subtracting within 1,000, with regrouping and word problems. The goal is fluency, not speed.

What you’ll learn

The skills inside this topic.

  • 01
    Add and subtract three-digit numbers

    Stack the place values and work right to left.

  • 02
    Regroup when a column overflows

    Carry a ten over, or borrow one from the next place.

  • 03
    Estimate sums and differences

    Round first, then add — useful as a sanity check.

  • 04
    Solve one and two-step word problems

    Read carefully and pick the operation.
